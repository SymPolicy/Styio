## Delivered Checkpoints

1. Q1 converged as allocation-free monomorphic callable values backed by final named function items and one complete invariant callable type.
2. Q4 converged as canonical closed/open callable effect rows in Sema, StyioIR, and the then-current schema-v3 interfaces; Q7 subsequently carried those facts into schema v4.
3. Q5 converged as compiler-owned canonical callable usage requirements with concrete-instance validation.
4. Q2 converged as exact explicit `$(...)` capture lists and an affine program-static scalar environment. Shared captures may escape under one concrete callable type, exclusive captures remain direct-call-only, and consume, imported, resource/container, or missing representation/drop environments fail before lowering.
5. Q7 converged as the schema-v4 split between a stable public callable contract and required canonical `styio.portable-styioir` schema-v1 bodies. The loader validates flat topological nodes, symbols, recomputed types, canonical bytes, semantic/dependency/compiler digests, and reconstructs imported definitions without parsing imported source or retaining a schema-v3 AST-text fallback.
